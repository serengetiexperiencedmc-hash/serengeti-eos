## 7. Provider response interpretation rules

Apply only to an **actual** provider reply recorded as a separate evidence receipt. Do not apply to this empty file.

A candidate does **not** need every SC question answered positively.

| Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Materially relevant Production hosting / managed-infrastructure proposition | The reply identifies a current or offered service that could host a business application and associated PostgreSQL workload, with enough identity (service name and/or official documentation) to make a full evidence request intelligible. |
| Principally out of EOS Production hosting scope | The relevant offering is principally connectivity, networking, cloud-connect, colocation **without** an applicable managed-hosting proposition, or another service outside EOS Production hosting scope. |
| Cannot establish the relevant offering | The reply does not establish a Production hosting / managed-infrastructure proposition, or no usable reply is obtained after an authorized send. |
| Marketing / public webpage | Not treated as verified capability. Official URLs in SC-02 are claims requiring later evidence. |
| Partner-delivered service (SC-03) | Does not by itself clear or close the gate. Record the boundary. Full RFI, if later eligible, must still evidence the operating party. |
| Planned / coming soon (SC-08) | Does not by itself close the gate. Record availability. A planned service may still be materially relevant; it is not treated as live Production capacity. |
| Geography (SC-04) | Relevance to Tanzania / Africa / EU questions only. Africa ≠ Tanzania. Location is not residency compliance and not architecture selection. |
| PostgreSQL / backup / support (SC-05–SC-07) | Useful for scope. Negative or incomplete answers do not automatically close the gate if a hosting proposition still exists; they remain items for a later full RFI. They also do not establish suitability. |

Do not infer suitability from a clarification reply. Do not treat a cleared candidate as meeting EOS Production requirements.
