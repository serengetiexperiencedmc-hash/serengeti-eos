**Company position (summary):** Internal Restricted class includes commercial contacts, RFPs, programmes, proposals, supplier/hotel commercial information and internal commercial documents. Restricted+/Highly Restricted candidates include identity/passport, health/accessibility, financial/payment, credentials and other highest-protection items. This is an **internal security/business classification**, not a claim of statutory special-category status.

**Counsel-style analysis**

SEDMC labels such as **Restricted**, **Highly Restricted** and **Restricted+** are **internal information-security classifications**. They must **not automatically be equated** with statutory categories of personal data, sensitive personal data, or GDPR special-category data.

Counsel-style mapping:

- No jurisdiction is **approved** or **forbidden** for Restricted data in an attested legal sense. Internal handling rules do not create a legal geography.

**Applicability classification:** Internal classification exists as company position — **CONFIRMED**. Equivalence to statutory sensitive/special-category data — **not automatic**; **REQUIRES FACT-SPECIFIC REVIEW**. Approved Restricted geography — **not recorded**.
