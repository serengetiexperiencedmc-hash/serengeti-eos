## 7. Evidence-Sufficiency Trigger

H-81 may move from `NOT STARTED — EVIDENCE TRIGGER NOT YET SATISFIED` to **substantive execution** only when there is **sufficient natural post-adoption evidence to make a meaningful qualitative assessment**.

This test is **deliberately qualitative**. There is **no** mandatory number of RFPs, opportunities, proposals, bookings, cases, days, weeks, or months, and **no** numerical evidence or maturity score.

Judgment across:

| Dimension | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **A. Genuine post-adoption activity** | Actual commercial activity after H-75 adoption exists. |
| **B. Process observability** | Evidence permits observation of meaningful portions of the adopted process, not merely that an enquiry existed. |
| **C. Evidence diversity** | Where naturally available, more than one stage may be illuminated (intake, qualification, classification, follow-up, proposal, approval, supplier costing, outcome). Diversity is **desirable**, **not** a numerical threshold. |
| **D. Recurrence** | Enough natural repetition to distinguish an isolated event from a recurring operating pattern. **No minimum number.** |
| **E. Distinguishability** | Reasonable distinction among process behaviour, evidence-access limitations, operating-practice gaps, and genuine capability needs. |
| **F. Commercial relevance** | A meaningful basis for whether a process gap or capability need matters to actual commercial operations. |
| **G. Path D relevance** | At least some assessment of whether a Path D candidate remains a future opportunity, is becoming a validated requirement, needs clarification, or may no longer be necessary. |
