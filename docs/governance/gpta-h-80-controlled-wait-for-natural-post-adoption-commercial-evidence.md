## 7. Controlled-Wait Principle

```text
WAIT FOR NATURAL EVIDENCE — DO NOT MANUFACTURE EVIDENCE
```

H-80 is **not** a requirement to wait for a predetermined number of days, weeks, months, RFPs, opportunities, proposals, bookings, or cases.

**Not authorized:**

- mandatory 30-day / 60-day / 90-day period
- minimum case / RFP / booking / proposal count
- numerical maturity threshold
- numerical evidence score

```text
NO NUMERICAL WAITING PERIOD IS AUTHORIZED
NO NUMERICAL EVIDENCE THRESHOLD IS AUTHORIZED
```

**Controlled wait means:**

1. H-75 remains the adopted commercial operating process.
2. Commercial activity continues through existing operational systems and practices.
3. Genuine commercial opportunities are handled normally.
4. No artificial cases are created for governance testing.
5. Evidence is reviewed only when genuinely available and accessible.
6. Operational sources remain outside EOS unless already authorized under H-70 Model B.
7. No connector, ingestion, migration, or application enhancement is introduced merely to make evidence easier to obtain.
8. When sufficient **natural** evidence becomes available, a subsequent governance review may assess process maturity and requirements again.
9. Until then, Path D remains requirements-only.
