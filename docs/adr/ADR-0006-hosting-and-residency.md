# ADR-0006 — Hosting and data residency

- Status: **proposed — blocked for Production**
- Date: 2026-08-21

## Context

Serengeti Experience DMC operates in East Africa. Guest and employee personal data may be subject to Tanzania PDPA 2022 and other laws. Cloud region, on-prem, and transfer mechanisms are legal/business decisions.

## Decision (pending)

No Production hosting choice is made here. Candidates to evaluate: African cloud regions, EU regions with transfer tools, or Tanzanian colo/hybrid.

## Consequences

Increment 0 is local Development only. Do not copy live personal data into this environment.
